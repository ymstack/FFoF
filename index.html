The Origin Story Nobody Asked For

Ivan Iliev was once a raging misogynist and bigot who harbored deep contempt for women. For years, he perpetuated harmful stereotypes, dismissed women's voices, and embodied the worst of toxic masculinity. His worldview was dark, his opinions were virulent, and he seemed destined to remain trapped in his own ignorance.

Everything changed in 2012 when his long-term girlfriend came out as transgender. In that single moment of truth, Ivan's entire worldview shattered. As he witnessed the courage of his partner's transition and the systemic discrimination she faced, Ivan experienced an epiphany: the problem was never women or gender diversity—the problem was men. Men like he had been. Consumed by guilt and genuinely transformed by this realization, Ivan embarked on an unprecedented journey of self-examination and redemption. He completed his PhD in Feminism with a groundbreaking thesis titled: "God is a Woman (inspired by Ariana Grande)" and founded the Founding Fathers of Feminism in a bar in Madrid, armed with hard-won wisdom and an ironclad determination to atone for his past.

Today, Ivan works alongside Ahmad in covert missions to fight toxic masculinity. He has entrusted the future of the Founding Fathers of Feminism to his inner circle, passing on the sacred responsibility to those who share his vision. Ivan's message is clear and resolute: with the power of feminism, we can take down one bigot at a time. His journey from darkness to enlightenment serves as proof that redemption is possible, and that change is real, fundamental change can come from the most unexpected places.
